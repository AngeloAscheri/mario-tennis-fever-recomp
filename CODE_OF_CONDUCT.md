# Code of conduct

mtf-recomp is a hobby project made by volunteers. To keep it a place people want to come back to:

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Everyone here gives their free time; newcomers are welcome, and so are
  questions that seem basic.
- **Criticise code and ideas, not people.** Reviews say what to change and why.
- **No harassment**, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in any language and in any space of the project
  (issues, pull requests, discussions, chats linked from here).
- **No piracy.** Do not ask for, offer or link game files, dumps, keys or builds made from the game. Those requests are
  closed without discussion.
- **Respect the maintainers' decisions** on scope and rules; disagree in the open, politely, with reasons.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, and block people who do not follow this. To report a problem
privately, contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile.

Inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
